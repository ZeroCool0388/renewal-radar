# Northwind Logistics Ltd — Services Agreement

## 1. Definitions and interpretation

In this Agreement, Business Day means a day other than Saturday, Sunday or a public holiday in the jurisdiction stated in clause 12. Customer Materials means information, specifications and content provided by the Customer for delivery of the Services. Confidential Information means information identified as confidential or which a reasonable commercial party would understand to be confidential, including service configurations, security controls, pricing and business plans. Deliverable means a document, configuration or other output expressly identified in an agreed statement of work. An Order is a written order executed by authorised representatives of both parties. Services means only the services described in clause 3 and the applicable Order.

References to clauses include schedules. Words in the singular include the plural. Headings assist navigation and do not affect interpretation. A reference to writing includes an electronic notice sent through the agreed contractual notice channel, but excludes an informal support ticket or chat message. An obligation not to do something includes an obligation not to permit it. Where a schedule conflicts with the body of this Agreement, the body prevails unless the schedule identifies the specific clause it overrides. Neither party may rely on a purchase order to vary these terms.

## 3. Services and delivery

The Supplier shall provide the Services with reasonable skill and care, using personnel with appropriate experience and training. The Services include implementation planning, configuration of the agreed environment, access to the contracted functionality and ordinary operational support. The parties shall agree milestones and acceptance criteria in writing before work begins. A feature described in a sales presentation is not a Deliverable unless included in an Order. The Customer shall provide timely access to required systems, accurate inputs and a designated commercial contact. Delay caused by missing dependencies will extend the affected milestone by the period of delay, subject to reasonable mitigation by the Supplier.

The Supplier shall maintain a service management process, record material incidents and provide monthly service reports. Planned maintenance shall normally take place outside the Customer's ordinary operating hours and shall be notified at least five Business Days in advance. The Supplier shall not materially reduce the core functionality of the Services during the contracted term. The Customer may not resell the Services, circumvent access restrictions or use the environment to process information outside the agreed purpose. Additional work requires a signed change request specifying the deliverable, price and delivery impact.

## 4. Service levels and governance

The parties shall meet quarterly to review service performance, open risks, change requests and forthcoming commercial deadlines. The Supplier shall nominate a service owner and the Customer shall nominate an operational owner. Neither nomination grants authority to amend this Agreement. Material service incidents must be acknowledged within four support hours and given an initial action plan within one Business Day. Routine support requests shall be acknowledged within two Business Days. Support hours run from 09:00 to 17:00 on Business Days unless an Order provides additional coverage.

The target monthly availability of the hosted service is 99.5%, excluding notified maintenance and events outside the Supplier's reasonable control. If availability falls below target, the Supplier shall investigate and provide a written corrective action plan. A service credit, where expressly agreed in an Order, is calculated against the monthly subscription charge for the affected service only. It does not replace the Customer's right to terminate for persistent material breach under clause 8. The Customer must submit a credit claim within thirty days after the relevant monthly report. No credit may be applied twice for the same incident.

Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information secure and use it only to perform or receive the Services. Disclosure is permitted only to personnel, professional advisers and permitted subcontractors who need to know and are subject to obligations no less protective than this clause. A party may disclose information required by law or a competent authority, provided that it gives advance notice where lawful and limits disclosure to what is required. Information is not confidential to the extent the receiving party can demonstrate that it was lawfully known, publicly available without breach, independently developed or received lawfully from a third party.

The Supplier shall not publish the Customer's name, logo or a case study without prior written consent for the specific publication. Consent may be withdrawn prospectively on reasonable notice. Confidentiality obligations survive for three years after expiry, and for as long as trade secrets remain trade secrets. On request after termination, a party shall return or securely destroy Confidential Information, except records retained under law, professional standards or ordinary backup cycles. Retained records remain subject to this clause and may not be used for any other purpose.

The required notice must reach the contractual notice contact no later than the deadline calculated by subtracting the notice period from the End date. Failure to give timely notice commits the parties to the renewal term on the existing terms, subject to a validly notified pricing amendment. Termination does not affect rights accrued before its effective date. The Customer shall pay charges for Services properly delivered up to that date. Clauses dealing with confidentiality, intellectual property, liability, accrued payment obligations and dispute resolution survive to the extent needed to give them effect. No termination or non-renewal notice is implied by a request for a quotation from an alternative supplier.

The Supplier shall ensure confidentiality, apply proportionate technical and organisational measures, maintain a record of subprocessors and notify the Customer of a suspected personal data breach without undue delay. ## 11. Business continuity and exit assistance

The Supplier shall maintain proportionate business continuity arrangements and test recovery procedures at least annually. Recovery testing must include restoration of a representative backup and review of dependency risks. The Supplier shall notify the Customer without undue delay when a material continuity event threatens delivery of the Services. Subcontracting does not relieve the Supplier of its contractual responsibilities. A material subcontractor with access to Customer information must be subject to suitable confidentiality and security controls.

For thirty days after expiry or termination, the Supplier shall make Customer data available for export in a commonly used machine-readable format, subject to payment of undisputed charges. Exit assistance beyond ordinary export is charged at the rates agreed in an Order and requires a written transition plan. The plan shall identify responsibilities, access arrangements, milestones and the date for final deletion. The Supplier shall not withhold data export solely because the Customer disputes an unrelated invoice in good faith. Deletion from active systems shall occur within sixty days after the export period; residual backups shall expire through the documented retention cycle.

The parties may agree mediation but neither party is required to accept a proposed settlement.
